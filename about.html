About Me

Amy is a 25 year old artist based in Guildford, living with cerebral palsy, epilepsy and autism. Her journey into the world of art began in 2019. After leaving school, she was struggling with her disabilities and feeling unsure about her future. After joining Artventure Trust in Guildford, Amy found her creative voice and started to experiment with different art styles and mediums. She found that art gave her a way to channel her ideas and express her unique perspective.

Amy loves painting with vibrant colours. Much of her work is created using vivid acrylics. She draws inspiration from nature, her travels and experiences and also takes inspiration from other artists. Her work captures fleeting moments or the essence of places she has been, and reimagines them through bold colours and thoughtful compositions.

Amy is proud of the unique style she has created and is excited to be taking part in the Surrey Artist of the Year 2026 exhibition.
