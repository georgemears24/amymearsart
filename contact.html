Sales: Terms and Conditions


Work marked as “Available” may be purchased. Please contact me (by email) to discuss. My prices generally range from £75 to £250 depending on the size of the work, whether it is canvas or canvas board, framed or unframed. I am not registered for VAT.

I do not arrange delivery of my work. It is available for collection in Guildford or you may arrange a courier at your own risk and expense. However, I do not sell to buyers outside the UK as there are legal and tax implications which I am not able to deal with.

The sale of a physical artwork does not transfer copyright or reproduction rights to the buyer. The buyer may not replicate or commercially exploit the artwork in any form.

I reserve the right to create up to two (2) subsequent original paintings that feature substantially similar subject matter, composition and style.
